GRASP

Design system & build brief

Everything needed to build the Grasp client: palette, type, components, chart specs, the full screen inventory, and how each screen maps to the Supabase schema. Visual direction is the porcelain palette with Fugaz One display type. Source of truth for behaviour remains the requirements spec (FR numbers are cited throughout).

01

Color

Porcelain ground, near-white surfaces, deep slate ink. Blue is the only brand accent and also means “solid”. Amber and red are reserved for retention health — they never decorate, so a warm color anywhere on screen always means something is slipping.

02

Fugaz One for the wordmark, screen titles and headline numbers only — it has one weight and no italics, so it must never carry running text. Work Sans does everything else. IBM Plex Mono carries small structural labels and intervals.

03

Layout, shape, elevation, motion

Spacing4 · 8 · 12 · 18 · 24 · 32. Screen gutter 24–26. Section gap 20. Nothing between 12 and 18.
RadiusCards 22 · controls 16 · pills 999 · badges 4. One radius per role, no mixing inside a screen.
ElevationOne shadow only: 0 20px 40px rgba(20,26,34,.12), on the active card. Everything else uses a hairline border.
Hit targetsGrading chips 64 tall, primary buttons 52, tab items 48. Nothing tappable under 44.
MotionReveal 180ms ease-out, card advance 240ms, swipe follows the finger with 12° max tilt. Ring and curve charts animate once on entry (600ms), never on scroll.
NoNo gradients on surfaces, no icon-only actions without a label, no streak or activity counter anywhere (FR-30).

05

Charts

Three chart forms, each with one job. Rings answer “where do I stand right now”, the curve answers “which way is it moving”, the bar row answers “which concept next”. No other chart types, no pie slices, no donut with more than two arcs.

DUAL RING — NAME vs EXPLAIN
CAN NAME79%
CAN EXPLAIN46%
Outer ring = anchor-card retrievability, inner = mechanism. The visible gap between arcs is the FR-28 name/mechanism gap. Inner arc takes the health color; outer is always accent. Stroke 11, cap round, starts at 12 o'clock.
90-DAY CURVE
NamingMechanism
Two series, weekly rollup, 90-day window. Only the naming series gets an area fill. Three gridlines (0 / 50 / 100). Hide entirely until 14 days of history exist — show “not enough history yet” instead (FR-30 defers long-range trends).

Every chart legend must name exactly the encodings drawn. If a series changes color by health, the legend becomes a three-step ramp key (slipping / softening / solid) instead of a series key.

06

Screen inventory

Fourteen screens for v1. Two tabs plus Session; Explore is phase 1b. Each entry lists what it holds, its states, and the requirements it satisfies.

01 · Dropbox connectONE-TIME · FR-1 → FR-3
One explanation, one button. States: disconnected · OAuth in progress · connected (shows vault path and note count) · scope error. The Full Dropbox scope requirement needs one plain sentence about why — the vault lives inside another app's folder.
02 · Session (question)DEFAULT LAUNCH · FR-16, FR-23
Opens straight into the stacked study card. Header: due / new counts and position. Card: reconstruct prompt, recall bead, question, concept name, tap to reveal. Footer: two-segment progress. No configuration, no note picker.
03 · Session (revealed)FR-22, FR-24, FR-25
Question shrinks to a header; the card holds the answer, type badge, and a link to the source note. Grading chips carry FSRS intervals. “Flag it” sits below, and flagging shows the rule in words: dislike means badly made, never hard.
04 · Source note sheetTRUST LAYER · FR-4
Bottom sheet showing the stored source excerpt with the note path. Read-only, with no edit affordance anywhere — the vault is never written to.
05 · Swipe vettingFR-14, FR-15, FR-20
A bounded batch woven into the session — the header states the count and a dot row shows what remains. Front and back both visible. Right accepts, left discards, up accepts then opens the wording editor; buttons mirror every swipe. Not an infinite feed.
06 · Wording editorFR-14 · SWIPE UP
Front and back fields, card type as a three-way selector, source excerpt visible below for checking against. Saves as approved.
07 · Session completeFR-15, FR-30
A stopping point, not a reward screen: what was reviewed, what was vetted, when the next cards come due, and one line telling the user to stop. No streak, no confetti, no “keep going”.
08 · RetentionTAB · FR-27 → FR-30
Dual ring with name / explain percentages, the 90-day curve, then concept rows sorted by widest gap. Answers what's solid, what's slipping, and where the gap is. Before 14 days of history: rings only.
09 · Concept detailFR-28, FR-29
Per-concept dual ring, a breakdown by card type, the cards most often missed, and “seen 47× / 60%” as a detail line with the reading that the cards are too fat. Times-seen never appears as a headline. Primary action: open the note.
10 · Remake pileFR-26
Flagged cards in a list with one batch action to regenerate. Reachable from Retention only, never surfaced mid-session. Regenerated cards re-enter through vetting.
11 · ExploreTAB · PHASE 1B · FR-19, FR-20
Adjacent concepts and web-sourced cards, each showing its citation inline — a card without a visible source is a bug. A single verify promotes into Retention; copy still points toward writing a note first.
12 · SettingsFR-17
New-card throttle (default 10) with a sentence on why raising it is a load tradeoff, plus a read-only line stating due reviews are never capped. Dropbox status, sign-out, generation mix read-out.
13 · Empty & error statesALL SCREENS
Nothing due today · no tagged notes yet · generation still running · Dropbox unreachable · not enough history for the curve. Each is one sentence and at most one action.
14 · Tab barFR-18, FR-19
Session · Retention · Explore. Sunk surface, hairline top border, active item in full ink. Hidden during an active session so the loop isn't interrupted.
07

Data to UI

Recall bead & health colorFSRS retrievability read from the card's serialized FSRS state. ≥80 accent · 50–79 softening · under 50 slipping.
Dual ringMean retrievability of anchor cards versus mechanism cards, rolled up by source note path.
CurveWeekly buckets of review logs joined to card type; a review counts as recalled at rating 3 or 4.
Grading intervalsThe four FSRS previews are computed before the chips render, so each interval is shown before it is chosen.
Session queueEvery approved card due now (uncapped), plus up to N new cards, plus a bounded slice of pending cards to vet.
Flag itMarks quality as disliked; an already-approved card also moves to the remake pile. It never touches FSRS state — the two signals stay on separate axes.
08

Voice

Coach-like and direct. The app says what to do next, then gets out of the way. It never congratulates, never counts sessions.
WRITE“You've been able to name it for three weeks. You still can't say why a Square breaks it.”
DON'T“Nice work! 7-day streak — keep the momentum going.”
Labels are nouns, not possessives (“Retention”, not “Your retention”). Every number carries a word saying what it measures. One sentence per empty state.
